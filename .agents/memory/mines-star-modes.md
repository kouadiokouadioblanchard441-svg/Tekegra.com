---
name: Mines star modes
description: Functional meaning of the two Premium Mines modes.
---

For Mines Premium, the Petite and Grosse choices refer only to the number of
recommended stars, never to betting odds or cote ranges. Petite generates 3–5
stars; Grosse generates 6–10 stars.

**Why:** Mines has no prediction multiplier, so describing these choices as
odds is misleading and does not match the intended user experience.

**How to apply:** Keep Mines labels and messages phrased around star counts;
do not reuse Lucky Jet’s cote terminology or validation for Mines.
